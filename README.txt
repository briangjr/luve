LUX v0.7.2 Pizza Jobs patch. Upload all three files to the root of your existing LUX GitHub repository, replacing index.html. Do not remove other files. Jobs tile appears under Get Paid on first load; tap it for the playable pizza conveyor game. Start with 4 pizzas/30 seconds, increasing quota/toppings and faster conveyor, health meter, flashing soda x2 bonus (0.85 second), cash payout when shift ends. This is an arcade prototype with illustrated canvas pizzas, not photorealistic assets. Keep your save data. JavaScript syntax checked; iPhone Safari not tested.
